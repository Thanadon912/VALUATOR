# Product

## Name

VALUATOR

## Register

product

## Users
Value investors performing repeatable company analysis. Optimize for traceability, comparison, and decision support rather than trading activity, social engagement, or financial entertainment.

## Product Purpose
A focused US equity research workspace for understanding market and valuation context, business revenue composition, supply-chain dependencies, financial statements, company profile, ownership structure, company news, and an editable DCF valuation.

## Brand Personality
Clean, strong, compact, and data-dense. Uses an almost-black background with a subtle green cast and very dark card surfaces. Strong white headings with muted green-gray supporting text.

## Anti-references
- No generic colors (plain red, blue, green) or uniformly green pages.
- No decorative orbs, bokeh, large gradients, floating shapes, or nested decorative cards.
- No "Related Tickers", Chart, Conversations, Historical Data, Analysis, or Options pages.
- No placeholder values presented as real values.
- No 2023-era kickers (small all-caps text above headings).

## Design Principles
- Facts, provider estimates, user assumptions, and app-derived calculations must be visually and structurally distinguishable.
- Every derived value must expose its calculation basis and source inputs.
- Keep every page scoped to the currently selected company.
- Missing values and negative values have distinct visual states.
- Financial tables must prioritize scanning over display typography.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Reduced-motion mode must show the final state without staged animation.
- Columns must remain aligned during loading.
