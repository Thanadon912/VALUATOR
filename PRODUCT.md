# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Value investors use VALUATOR to repeatedly research a selected company, compare valuation methods, and return to saved stocks across devices. AI agents consume the same source-aware valuation outputs through deterministic JSON APIs.

## Product Purpose

VALUATOR is a universal fair-value workspace and API. It helps users inspect current company evidence, run five valuation engines, understand calculation basis and uncertainty, and avoid treating unavailable or placeholder data as fact.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, and instrument-like. The interface should feel calm under dense financial information and confident without becoming promotional or theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS onboarding, uniformly green dashboards, decorative sci-fi control rooms, oversized marketing typography, unexplained financial jargon, and interfaces that blur provider facts, estimates, user assumptions, or derived calculations.

## Design Principles

1. Make source quality and unavailable states explicit.
2. Keep the selected company and the user's current analytical task in view.
3. Reward repeated use with compact, predictable controls and saved context.
4. Reserve visual emphasis for actions, selection, status, and financial meaning.
5. Prefer earned trust and clear recovery over promotional persuasion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-only operation, visible focus states, semantic labels and status messages, WCAG AA text contrast, 44-pixel touch targets where practical, browser zoom, and reduced-motion preferences. Never rely on color alone to communicate state.
